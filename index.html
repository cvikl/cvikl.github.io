Legis Labs builds a verification engine for regulated work. It checks AI output against its sources, line by line, over a dependency graph, so that people can trust what the model produced. We are in Accelerate Cambridge, Cohort 40, at Cambridge Judge Business School.

In 2023 I co-founded Marcelino Technologies, which builds AI and SaaS for more than ten industrial clients, including EasyTask and iDrive. I founded FRI Founders, the startup community at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science in Ljubljana. I now advise both and remain a shareholder in Marcelino.

I study Computer Science on the AI track at the University of Ljubljana, with an exchange semester at the University of Oklahoma. My work spans AI verification, engineering, product in regulated domains, and research in NLP and computer vision.

Outside of code, I take the gym seriously because consistency there carries over everywhere else.

I am the co-founder of Legis Labs. We build a verification engine for regulated work. It checks AI output against its sources, line by line, so that people can trust what the model produced. We are in Accelerate Cambridge, and the engine is live on UK immigration case preparation.

Before that I co-founded Marcelino Technologies in Celje and founded FRI Founders, the startup community at the Faculty of Computer and Information Science (FRI) in Ljubljana. I now advise both.

I study Computer Science on the AI track at the University of Ljubljana, with an exchange semester at the University of Oklahoma.

Outside of code, I take the gym seriously because consistency there carries over everywhere else.

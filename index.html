Recent projects

Agent Passport

Making delegated AI-agent payments safer: an Agent Passport and communication standard for agent identity, authority and permissions. My focus was the technical architecture, implementation and turning the concept into a working prototype. Winner of the $25,000 award at the inaugural CDIR Global Agentic Regulator Hackathon, from 942 builders and 336 team submissions across 65+ countries.

Vigilance

A clinician-in-the-loop clinical audit tool on Google's MedGemma. The model reads patient records and surfaces the events an audit needs, and a clinician verifies every judgement against the source. It runs locally, so patient data never leaves the hospital. People's Choice Award, Adeline Health x AI Hackathon, Google HQ London, 2026.

Hack the Law

Representing Slovenia at the University of Cambridge against students from Harvard, MIT, Oxford and Cambridge. We reinvented how legal documents are represented, an approach that is genuinely novel with very little existing research behind it. Winner of the Confidence in AI challenge, sponsored by Luminance, 2026.
